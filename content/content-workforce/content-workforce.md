> **Index:** [[index]] · **Commercial:** [[commercial]]

This section tracks the **configuration work**: the interviews that produce TXN's content manifesto, content pillars and brand entities, and the state of each output as it is built.

Scope note: this section records **the work and its outputs**. Interview transcripts are not held here. Discussion in the sessions is candid by design and belongs with the engagement material, not in the product vault.

## Method

Max pre-loads everything known about the business, including TXN's own supplied documents, and generates a first draft of each output. The session is then a **refinement workshop** against that draft rather than a day-long interview from a blank page. Roughly an hour per session instead of four.

Validated in session 1 and continuing on that basis. The trade-off to watch: sessions over-run, so the booked slots are being used in sequence rather than each output finishing in one sitting.
