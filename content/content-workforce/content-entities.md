# TXN — Content Entities

> **Section:** [[content-workforce]]
> **Status:** Drafted, issued for async review after Interview 1
> **Last worked:** Interview 1, 19 August 2026

An entity is a configured voice: what it sounds like, what it cares about, and who it is speaking to. TXN has four.

## The four entities

| Entity | Type | Agenda |
|--------|------|--------|
| **TXN** | Company | Prescriptive: the product, the offer, what it does for the customer |
| **Ian Johnson** | Individual | Thought leadership |
| **Michael Moores** | Individual | Thought leadership |
| **Dorte Dye** | Individual | Thought leadership |

Individual profiles are included at no additional charge. Each connects back to the company through the shared TXN pillar, so an individual can write in the company register when that is what the post calls for.

## Decisions taken

- **Agenda is a balance**, not a single mode. Descriptive content establishes credibility by teaching and informing; prescriptive content says the product is the right one. The three individuals lean thought leadership, the company leans prescriptive.
- **Resharing company posts is not thought leadership.** A reshare with a short added comment communicates nothing about the person. Company content should still be reshared for network reach, but it does not count as the individual's own voice.
- **Credibility is not the opening line.** The founding story and the shareholder background are a reason to believe, reached once someone is already interested. The lead has to be the problem being solved and the value delivered.
- **Personal and company voices must not contradict.** If an individual's positioning pulls against the company's, the audience is left asking which one is real.
