# Source Documents

TXN's own go-to-market corpus, as delivered. Routed from [[outbound]].

The distinguishing fact of this engagement is that **TXN arrived with GTM material already written.** Ian Johnson authored this library between April and August 2026. The onboarding work is reconciliation and enhancement, not authoring from scratch, and that is only true because these documents exist.

## How to use this directory

`originals/` holds every file exactly as Ian sent it, unmodified. The markdown files beside it are verbatim conversions, made so the corpus is searchable, wikilinkable and readable in the vault rather than locked inside Word. **Where a conversion and its original disagree, the original governs.**

Five of Ian's documents already had a home in the vault before this: [[icp-definition]], [[prospecting-process]], [[persona-champion]], [[persona-primary-user]] and [[persona-economic-buyer]], plus the analysis of the scored register at [[qualification-matrix]]. Those are not duplicated here as markdown; their originals sit in `originals/` alongside the rest.

## Reading order Ian recommends

[[outbound-content-playbook]] is the entry point he wrote for a delivery partner, and it consolidates the rest. Read it first, then go to the source document behind whichever section matters, because the playbook is derivative by design and the sources govern.

## Governance

Ian Johnson owns this library. His standing instruction is to propose changes to him rather than deviate quietly. TXN is pre-launch, and several claims across the corpus are explicitly marked as hypothesised or as engineered targets rather than proven outcomes. Those markings must survive into anything the workforce sends.
