# P-1 Digital banking

> **Up:** [[market-profiles]]. **What this is:** firmographic criteria that we source leads with; the workforce then researches each company and ranks it against the ICPs. Card program status is not here, because no lead source holds it.
>
> **Tags.** `[Ian]` = Ian ruled it, with the place. `[TXN]` = TXN's own documents. `[Derived]` = our inference, for Ian to confirm. `[Gap]` = not known yet.

**Who it finds.** Companies whose product is an account: neobanks, digital banks for consumers and businesses, crypto platforms that convert to spendable money, and (pending Ian) banks. Ian counts these as card-essential: without a card, the product looks odd `[Ian, 3 Sep L901]`. TXN's Core Messaging names this cluster first and calls it "the highest-priority sub-sector" `[TXN, core-messaging.md §3.1]`.

**Feeds:** ICP 1 (no card yet), ICP 2 (live card, new product or market), ICP 4 when it is active. The research decides which.

## Company types

- Status: Private, Public `[Ian, §4.6]`.
- Type: FinTech (a Prospeo company type) for neobanks and crypto platforms `[Derived]`.
- Ownership: any. Ian's list (VC-backed, PE-controlled, listed) describes who is in scope; it is not a filter `[Ian, §4.6]`. A bootstrapped, bank-owned or unrecorded company stays in.
- Tier 1 banks are out, but a Tier 1 bank's digital subsidiary is not excluded in advance `[Ian, §7; 2 Sep L648-656]`.

## Headcount

**Any** `[Ian]`. Size rules no one out `[Ian, 2 Sep L852]`, and headcount *"doesn't warrant a significant score"* `[Ian, 3 Sep L909]`. Companies with fewer than 10 people are in scope.

## Funding stage

**Any. No funding filter.** TXN's Core Messaging: *"the primary segmentation axis is sub-sector, not company stage"* `[TXN, core-messaging.md:68]`. Ian removed the capital test because raises are often undisclosed `[Ian, 3 Sep L965-991]`. A funding filter would also drop every company with no recorded round.

## Keywords

**Guide terms, not strict filters.** The sourcing agent may drop, add or change terms, and widen a search that returns too few fitting companies. An exclude term is a reason to check a company, not to drop it: a fitting company that mentions, for example, its accounting integrations stays in.

|---|---|---|
| Digital banking for consumers | neobank, digital bank, challenger bank, mobile bank, current account, e-money account, debit card | core banking software, banking software vendor, bank consultancy |
| Digital banking for businesses | business account, SME banking, business banking app, multi-user business account | accounting software, bookkeeping services |
| Crypto with fiat rails | crypto exchange, crypto wallet, crypto card, buy crypto, crypto to fiat, off-ramp, stablecoin payments | mining, NFT, DeFi protocol, blockchain development |
| Banks | savings bank, cooperative bank, regional bank, retail bank | investment bank, private bank, central bank |
