# P-4 Card-adjacent businesses

> **Up:** [[market-profiles]]. **What this is:** firmographic criteria that we source leads with; the workforce then researches each company and ranks it against the ICPs. Card program status is not here, because no lead source holds it.
>
> **Tags.** `[Ian]` = Ian ruled it, with the place. `[TXN]` = TXN's own documents. `[Derived]` = our inference, for Ian to confirm. `[Gap]` = not known yet.

**Who it finds.** Businesses outside core finance that hold a customer relationship and move money to or from their customers, in verticals where peers already run card programs: trading and investment apps, payout and disbursement businesses, retailers and e-commerce companies. Ian counts these as card-adjacent `[Ian, 3 Sep L901; §4.2.1]`, and bounds the list to verticals where card programs are known to exist `[Ian, 3 Sep L653-657]`.

**Feeds:** ICP 3 (no card, a peer runs one). A company here with a live card goes to ICP 2 (a visible new product or market) or ICP 4 (none). The research decides which. Every vertical carries every ICP; some verticals here hold no first-program company `[Ian, 21 Oct L681-691]`.

## Geography

The same as [[market-profile-1-digital-banking|P-1]], and a guide, not a strict filter (search by where the company operates, not only by its HQ): EEA and UK, in Ian's phase order (Poland, Czech Republic, Romania, Hungary first) `[Ian, §4.4]`. Germany LinkedIn only `[TXN, prospecting-process.md:319]`. TXN contracts only with companies in Europe or the UK `[Ian, 21 Oct L637]`; a company with no entity there takes an anti-signal in every ICP, on evidence only. Published card issuance outside Europe and the UK, or for Ukraine, is a mark-down in each ICP, not an exclusion `[Ian, 21 Oct L641-657]` · Ukraine `[Derived]`.

Investment Management; Capital Markets; Financial Services; General Retail; Online and Mail Order Retail `[Ian, §4.1]`, written as Prospeo industry values. The narrower retail industries (for example Retail Apparel and Fashion) can be added per run `[Derived]`.
