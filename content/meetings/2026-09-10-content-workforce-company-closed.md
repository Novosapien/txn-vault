> **Source:** Gemini transcript, 10 September 2026, 10:00 BST, 28m. **Max Kingaby** with **Ian Johnson** and **Dorte Dye**; **Lily St. Clair** present. **Brett absent**, as were Tyler and George, all travelling.

## The company content is closed, by adopting Ian's personal output

The session was meant to finish the entity section of the company interview and then leave one more session for the pillars. Neither happened, for a good reason.

Ian had completed his personal onboarding the day before, and fed TXN's existing brand and tone-of-voice work into it:

> *"When I finished off my own personal one... basically what I did was fed in the work that we've already done as a business on our brand work and tone of voice. So that's kind of my personal one, but I think it's important that that is the one that gets used here rather than go back over... How we want to talk as a company is already in my personal one."*

And his personal content has no separate subject from the company's:

> *"My content will be about, not necessarily about the company, but about the things that are tangential to TXN... any topic I post about is within the kind of environment of where we sit as a company. I've got nothing to say about [anything else]."*

**Max's resolution, accepted:** take everything Ian produced, manifesto, entity and his three pillars, and put it into the TXN business page as the company workforce. *"That saves us an hour doing the entity bit."* Ian had already reviewed it: *"I went through it thoroughly and checked and I was happy with it. So you can just put it straight in."*

Ian closed it: **"for now, we don't need to carry on with this. I think you guys upload it and then we've got a default position."**

**So the gate at [[open-questions]] #81 is cleared**, not by holding the session but by discovering its output already existed. The upload is Max's, once landed.

### What Ian's route means for the company entity

**The company voice is now built from one person's onboarding.** That is defensible here, since Ian fed in the business's own brand and tone work rather than his personal voice, and Ian has led TXN's messaging since before the engagement. But it means the company entity inherits whatever the personal interview asked Ian, and the next section records that it asked company questions of everyone.

## The interview design flaw reproduces, and it is not drift

**This reframes [[open-questions]] #86.** On 8 September Dorte reported that her personal interview drifted onto company material, and George's hypothesis was cross context pollution. Ian has now reported the same thing independently:

> *"I have to say that the questions that I got in the process I went through were very much company related. There was no way of distinguishing between the two. Maybe that's because I was thinking about the company."*

Dorte, immediately: *"No, but I had the same, that's why I stopped, because I felt like I went the wrong turn."*

**Two people, two separate sessions, the same result.** That is not pollution in one run, it is the design: **the personal onboarding asks company questions and has no way to separate the person from the business.** Ian offered the charitable reading that it was his own framing; Dorte's identical experience rules that out.

For Ian the flaw happened to produce the right output, since he has no separate subject. **For Dorte it produced the wrong one**, because she does: she stopped, and she and Max rebuilt her structure by hand.

### The structure that came out of it

| Person | Structure | Reason |
|--------|-----------|--------|

| **Dorte** | **A business pillar** plus **a private pillar** that still covers operations but not TXN operations | *"to just get more traffic to me and then to the company"*, and her personal posts outperform company posts ([[content-pillars]]) |
| **Michael** | **Not yet onboarded** | The only personal workforce outstanding |

Max on the optional pillar: *"it's not a separate interview process, it's just one ten minute session, just in case you want to post about stuff outside of TXN."* Ian wants it but explicitly at lower priority: *"I just want to prioritise this one at the moment."*

**Dorte asked the right alignment question:** *"do we need to align my business one then with that one as well? Shouldn't we have then all the same business one, or should there be differences?"* Max: *"it's more authentic to your audience to have your own view, your own perspective on the business, as long as it doesn't misalign with the business itself."* Resolution: **cross-check Dorte's business pillar against Ian's company one.**

### The personal workforces are one self-onboarding each, not six interviews

The vault has recorded since 27 August **two personal interviews each** for Ian, Michael and Dorte, six in total. In practice **each person completes a self-onboarding tool once**, and a dashboard appears at the end. Ian and Dorte both finished on 9 September.

**Ian's experience of the end of it is a UX finding:** *"at the end of it a dashboard just popped up with a bunch of stuff."* Max: *"I get that can be somewhat confusing just landing upon this random dashboard and not really know much about it. That's really useful feedback in itself."* His proposed fix is an automated **"this is how it works" video** at the end of onboarding.

## Ian wants the outputs now, to keep the message consistent

Ian's reason is concrete and it is about launch, not curiosity:

> *"What we want to do is make sure that the output that comes from these sessions, particularly the one that I've done and the company one, is then fed into our own internal processes, to make sure that any messaging that goes from anywhere or any collateral that gets produced is on message."*

Two named external parties now need it:

- **A head of marketing from Pay Corp**, helping with the website
- **A PR consultant**, met on Tuesday 8 September, who will help get TXN's message into publications

Ian could not find a way to export it from the platform himself.

**Max cautioned against sharing a first draft**, and it is the honest caveat:

> *"Quite often, and this is the reason why we've introduced Tyler into the product, is that first hit, it's okay. It's not where ideally we'd want it to be. So what we do is Tyler works on it with you... the output that you're giving to them probably won't be as good as what it will be in say a couple of weeks' time."*

**Ian overruled it, with a sound reason:** *"we're trying to plan back from a launch date, and there's quite a lot of stuff that needs to get done, but refining stuff once it's built is a lot easier than kind of trying to get everything done from scratch at the very last minute."* He will share it with the caveat attached.

**Delivered during the call.** Max passed the markdown versions to Lily, who converted them to Word and emailed them to Ian and Dorte: Ian's manifesto, his entity, and his three pillars, in that order.

## Ian asks how honest TXN's AI claims can be

The most consequential part of the session, and it is not about content at all.

Ian sent Brett an email on 9 September asking for Novosapien's view on how TXN should talk about AI. He explained it on the call.

### What he said

> *"There's this whole thing about people go out and say 'oh, we're AI native, it's an AI layer, it's AI enabled, it's AI this.' And what I've said to Brett is, we just need Novosapien's view, knowing what you're building for us: what's the best way of talking about how we're using AI in our company?"*

> *"I don't want to just throw AI out as a term left, right and centre for the sake of it, because literally everybody is doing that, and I know 99% of them are talking utter [nonsense]."*

> **"We're not an AI native company. Let's be really clear about this.** As I understand AI native, it's not pervasive in everything we do. The platform hasn't been built thinking about AI first. Obviously the stuff we're doing with you guys is, because that's what it is."*

> *"We need to make sure that whatever we're saying is backed up by what's being delivered."*

### The rule he set for launch

> *"For absolute sure, when we launch this, not everything that we're planning to do will be available. But if we make grand claims about stuff in our content and then the reality is that it's not there, that's going to be really difficult. If we state what our philosophy is, and where we're headed, and what we're building, and then we say what we've built already, that's a completely different way of doing it."*

**So the positioning has three layers, and they must not be merged:** philosophy and direction, what is being built, and what is built. Only the last may be claimed as available.

### What he wants from Novosapien specifically

Not messaging. An assessment:

> *"We need a real external expert's view on what we're building with you and where that puts us, when we think about the AI element of it in particular."*

> *"The sense checking to it is, as somebody who's in the AI industry: not how we should position it from a messaging perspective, but really **how much value and how far have we gone in terms of what we can offer**. That's the difference."*

And he wants it to be *"a really honest view."*

### And he tied it to the next phase of spend

> **"If what we're delivering is not moving the needle enough, then we need to go back and think about how we use the next phase of the Novosapien money to make sure that we are building stuff that does move the needle.** Not just for the sake of it, but it's delivering value."*

**This is the client asking Novosapien to assess the value of its own delivery, and saying in the same breath that the answer governs the next phase of the budget.** It follows directly from Ian's 3 September critique of the interaction model ([[2026-09-03-agentic-standup]]), where his test was how much value the agent adds over doing the task by hand. It is a fair question, it is a phase-two risk, and it needs an honest answer rather than a positioning document. Raised at [[open-questions]] #88.

### Why this is sharper than it sounds: a claim is already out of line

[[offer]] already carries the right rule, set by Ian on 24 August: *"AI is the mechanism that delivers outcomes; it is never TXN's identity claim, and messages never lead with the technology."* So Ian's stance is consistent, not new.

**But TXN's own Product Overview v1.0 lists, as a Console capability, "Build card products manually or with AI-assisted (agentic) setup"** ([[source-documents]]). That is **guided product launch**, which TXN ranked **tenth of ten** in its own workflow slate on 25 August, with **two of six** tools covered and flagged as *"the largest build on the slate"* ([[2026-08-25-workflow-slate-decision]]). It is not in the pilot.

That is exactly the claim Ian is worried about: a capability described as available in TXN's own collateral that the agentic work does not deliver at launch. And TXN's Core Messaging v2.5 lists **"Agentic"** among its approved *"words to use."* Neither is wrong as philosophy; both are wrong as a statement of what is built.

**Dorte made the same point about delivery before content:** *"I meant delivery. What you're building for us AI-wise on the platform versus what we are talking about. I think that's the main thing, right?"*
