# TXN: Domains and the September Schedule (2026-09-04)

> **Source:** Gemini transcript, 4 September 2026, 14:00 BST, 30m. Brett StClair, Max Kingaby, Tyler Thompson and Lily St. Clair, with **Dorte Dye**; **George Westbrook** joined for the domain block and left.
>
> **The content workshop did not happen.** It was booked and recorded as **Content Workshop 3**. Ian could not dial in: *"Ian's got a power cut at his house at the moment with all those builders and stuff."* Brett, at 00:00: *"it doesn't look like we're going to do the content workforce."* The hour became domains, the September diary and UAT.
>
> **That is the fifth displacement of the content work.** Unlike the previous four this one is nobody's decision, but the count is what matters and the consequence is the same.

## Domains: the arrangement is settled

Dorte came to the call for one thing, and she was explicit about why: *"I literally just need a short this is the ask I can give to Ian. Ian is in view of it because he has a fancy budget and it's all about making sure the budget is covered."*

She was also clear this is a **housekeeping requirement, not a doubt about the work**: *"when we split responsibilities, because everything else is done by Pay, it makes perfectly sense that you guys are doing that for us. But we just need to have the housekeeping exercise on top of that."*

### The mechanism

| Item | Position |
|------|----------|
| **Who buys** | **Novosapien.** Registered on a new account created for the purpose, not on an existing Novosapien one |
| **How TXN gets them** | Login details handed over, so TXN holds the account outright |
| **Billing** | Novosapien pays on card, then invoices TXN |
| **Uplift** | **Costs passed through at a 10% markup** |
| **Constraint** | It must be built **in a transferable state** from the start. Brett: *"we just got to make sure we've got it into a transferable state"* |
| **Open** | The handover route itself. Brett proposed a Gmail account; Dorte queried it (*"I have no clue how that works. I'm just wondering why Gmail"*) and George took it away: *"I'll work out the best way to hand over accounts"* |

George also drew the distinction that matters for the handover: **domains renew yearly and email accounts do not**, so the two may want different homes. *"It might be better for domains [to] do theirs and email accounts... domains obviously they're done on a yearly basis."*

### TXN owns exactly one domain

A firmer constraint than the vault has recorded, and it came out of George asking what the main domain is.

> Dorte: *"We only have one, and that's TXN Global. There are a couple of other TXNs where they have taken some... and that goes to Pay at the moment. It's redirected. So as far as I'm aware, and I can double check, we only have secured txnglobal.com."*

So the sending domains cannot be near-variants of a family TXN already controls, because there is no family. Every other TXN name is held by a third party and redirects to Pay Corp. That shapes what the four or five sending domains can look like and is worth settling before purchase rather than after.

### What George owes, and by when

He committed on the call and left immediately to do it: *"I'll literally get on to that now... it'll be there by the end of today."*

The deliverable, as Brett specified it:

- The **steps** to be taken
- **Indicative costs** per domain, checked rather than estimated
- **Quantities**, and confirmation of how many email accounts are needed
- A price for the email accounts, obtained rather than assumed
- The **one domain to three email accounts** ratio, which is all the structure Dorte needs (*"send one domain, you have three email accounts linked to this, this is all I need to know"*)
- **The warming urgency stated on top.** Dorte: *"the urgency is that we need to warm up ideally next week... If you put that on top, perfect. Because then we know we need to get our ducks in a row"*

**The deadline is real and it is external.** Dorte is with Ian at **08:00 on Monday 7 September** and wants the thumbs up in that meeting: *"If you have that ready by then, then I will have hopefully the thumbs up by that time."*

> **This is the third deadline on this item.** It was owed on 2 September, again on 3 September (*"George was meant to write it up last night"*), and is now owed by end of 4 September against a client meeting. The running joke on the call was about exactly this, Dorte on *"English now"* versus *"German now"*, and George: *"not a South African now, which means I'll do it."* Recorded because the thing being joked about is a two-week clock that has not started.

### Warming

Brett's target: *"we'll have to aim for somewhere mid next week, mid following week."* Dorte confirmed the shape she expects, *"two weeks warming up the emails and then that time."*

**Mid next week plus two weeks lands the domains live around the end of September**, which is inside the retreat. That is the outcome the 27 August decision was taken to avoid.

### The final content workshop is scheduled into the departure

This is the finding, not the booking.

The 10 September session is **the one that unlocks TXN's access to the Content Workforce platform**. It is scheduled at 10:00 on the morning the Novosapien team flies. Tyler's flight is at 11:00 South African time, *"so I'm assuming that's 10 UK time"*, and he wants to be at the airport early because it is OR Tambo. Max was visibly reluctant and Brett noticed (*"I'm still trying to figure out why Max can't do 10 o'clock on Thursday"*); Max's answer was that he can run it.

Every alternative was tested on the call and closed:

- **Monday 7 Sep, 14:00** was Ian's own content workshop; running two back to back was rejected on quality grounds. Dorte: *"you need to have a fresh brain. Don't make these big sessions block to block."* Max agreed: *"you just get bored and you rush it."*
- **Tuesday and Wednesday** are out because Ian is in London, and Dorte deliberately protected that time: *"I just don't want to take more time available from Ian when he is in London."*
- **Wednesday early** was blocked by Dorte's physiotherapy appointment, already rescheduled once.
- **Friday** was rejected because nobody knew landing times.

So the slot is genuinely the last one, and it carries a single point of failure: **Brett noted that everything sits on Max's machine**, and Max is the one flying with the least margin.

> **Recorded at [[open-questions]] #81.** Dorte left the door open, *"if we can get you another earlier slot, then we book it"*, so the mitigation is to find a Wednesday slot rather than to accept the Thursday one.

## Dorte's horoscope problem

The best product finding in the session, and it is a genuine one about how personas are elicited rather than a complaint.

She started her personal profile and stopped:

> *"I've started to run your profile, but I got distracted yesterday with the meeting, and actually Claude asked me so many questions. What I wanted to do is a little bit of a decision tree to see what different outcomes are, because half of them it is more like you're doing a horoscope. You feel today in that mood and tomorrow I'm taking something completely different."*

Brett recognised it immediately: *"That's a good point. Yeah, that's a really good point."*

**Her proposed fix is better than the complaint.** She will complete it, but record exactly what she selected, so the run can be repeated against different parameters:

> *"I will do it, but when I do it then I know exactly what I have ticked so that we can run it later and do it with one of the other parameters."*

**Why this matters beyond Dorte.** The personal content workforces elicit tone and preference through a long question set, and if the answers are mood-dependent then the persona is a snapshot of a Tuesday rather than a stable profile. Six of these interviews are planned, two each for Ian, Michael and Dorte. If the instrument is unstable, running it twice per person does not fix it, it just produces two snapshots.

Recorded at [[open-questions]] #82. Dorte will have hers ready for Monday.

## Knowledge hub over console

The most consequential technical statement in the call, and it came in the last four minutes.

Dorte asked whether Novosapien is aligned with Stackworkz. Brett's answer:

> *"you know what I'm going to do is harass the crap out of George because I bet he hasn't shared the code with him yet."*

So the code-sharing plan opened on 26 August ([[2026-08-26-stackworkz-agent-demo]]) has **not been actioned**, nine days on. Then Dorte set the priority:

> *"that's the biggest thing, for the knowledge hub. You both need to be aligned. **Console is not as important. Console is working in the background. But if you want to launch everything, knowledge hub needs to be running.**"*

**This reframes [[open-questions]] #62.** That row asks how the agentic surface reaches the **Stackworkz Console**, and every wire-in discussion to date has been console-shaped. Dorte has just said the console is the part that is fine and the **knowledge hub** is the launch blocker.

It is consistent with two things already in the vault and neither was connected to this. Michael said on 25 August that he was in *"full UAT for knowledge hub and the API right now... that has to be done before I go"* ([[2026-08-25-agentic-standup]]). And [[commercial]] records the Knowledge Hub as **TXN's first externally written material**, with Michael's draft content awaiting approval for ingestion.

**What is not clear** is what "knowledge hub needs to be running" requires from Novosapien specifically: whether it is the [[knowledge-engine]] work, the content ingestion, the agent's ability to answer from it, or simply the Stackworkz code hand-off unblocking Michael. Nobody asked on the call. Raised as part of #62 rather than as a new row, because it changes that question rather than adding one.
