## The headline: the session did not do what it was booked to do

The invitation named this **Interview 2, Brand Entity**. No brand entity work happened. The session opened where session 1 stopped, in the middle of the manifesto's central-conflict section, and spent the full hour finishing the manifesto.

That is not a failure of the session. The manifesto is now **complete through every section**, which it was not on 19 August, and Ian worked it hard. But it means **two outputs slipped**: the content pillars, which session 1 had already deferred, and the brand entity, which this session was booked for.

Brett closed the session by naming both explicitly:

> "Can I caveat that's the end of it **of the content manifesto**. So what we need to find some time with you is to work through the **content pillars** ... and then we need about half an hour to do your **entity**."

Both are booked for **Thursday 27 August**.

## What the session produced

The manifesto was reviewed section by section against Max's pre-loaded draft, in the refinement format validated in session 1. Ian led, with Dorte on specific wording. The decisions taken:

| Section | Decision |
|---------|----------|
| **Central conflict** | The expertise framing has to **bridge both audience categories**, not split them. Ian rejected a version implying a business that must have a card "hires the expert it never wanted": those organisations generally do employ payments people, they are just not card specialists, so the value is **limiting how much card-specific expertise is needed from day one**. Splitting every persona into a version A and a version B is explicitly to be avoided |
| **Central conflict** | **Cut the "AI versus human / capability without comprehension" line.** Ian: *"that just doesn't make any sense to me. There's no value to having that in the manifesto"* |
| **Vision of the future** | **Reorder the interaction models so the bolder claim leads.** "We propose, you approve" comes before "you drive, we advise", because the first is TXN bringing improvements it has identified from the data, and the second is the client doing the thinking |
| **Vision of the future** | **"Your agent talks to ours" is one model of three, not the model.** The other two are building straight to the APIs, and using the console with or without the co-pilot. Meeting the client where they are is the principle, and the draft read as though agent-to-agent was the only route |
| **Vision of the future** | Cut **"blast radius"**. Ian: *"particularly hate the expression."* Also cut the first sentence of point one as redundant |
| **Core promise** | Broadly accepted. Cut **"you do not arrive expert"**, which neither Ian nor Dorte could parse |
| **Target audience** | Three personas generated, up from the usual two, and accepted. Two corrections. The technical persona is **not the decision maker but a significant influencer**, and is never simply "handed a vendor and a deadline": Ian has never had a client where the integrator was outside the evaluation. And their fear is not only their own workload, it is **picking a platform their team will hate working with**, the developer tools and the APIs included |
| **Core tenets** | Points 1, 2 and 4 fine. **Point 3 rewritten to an earned-trust model** and Ian liked the result: permissions widen as trust is built, so *"suspending one card and terminating 400 must never share a permission model"* holds on day one but may not hold forever. Brett's phrase **"an earned trust model"** is the one Ian endorsed. **Points 5 and 7 cut** as adding nothing. **Point 6 kept**, built AI-native against retrofitted, with the console example queried by Dorte |
| **Stakes and call to arms** | **Do not disparage "AI added to consoles built for specialists"**, because TXN does exactly that with its own co-pilot. Nothing in the manifesto may make one of the three interaction models sound inferior |
| **Stakes and call to arms** | **Cut the attributed quote from Ian** about moving fast and staying ahead. It is internal and of no interest externally. The position that replaces it: TXN will **push the boundary of the category but never ship unproven AI capability**, because *"operationally it doesn't work and causes a whole heap of issues"* |
| **Style** | **No em dashes** in any generated content, instructed live into the engine |
