Data Processing Agreement
Knowledge Hub AI Assistant: Interim Hosting

Dated             2026 · between Novosapien (Novosapien Global Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company no. 17308181, registered office Aldgate Tower, 2 Leman Street, London E1 8FA) and TXN (TXN Global Limited, a company incorporated in Cyprus, company no.         , registered office Office 303, 20 Iacovou Patatsou, Egkomi, 2408 Nicosia, Cyprus).

Background. TXN operates a Knowledge Hub for developers. Novosapien built the AI assistant that answers questions on it. Until the assistant moves to hosting provided by Direct Transact, Novosapien hosts it in Novosapien's own Microsoft Azure subscription. In doing so Novosapien processes a limited amount of personal data on TXN's behalf. This Agreement sets the terms for that processing, and meets Article 28 of the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR.

1. What this Agreement covers

1.1 The Interim Service is the Knowledge Hub documentation co-pilot and the documentation server, hosted by Novosapien in Novosapien's own Azure subscription in the North Europe (Ireland) region, from the Knowledge Hub launch until Production Cutover. Production Cutover is the point, confirmed in writing by both parties, at which the assistant moves to hosting in the TXN Azure subscription operated by Direct Transact.

1.2 What the Interim Service does not touch. The Interim Service answers from TXN's public documentation only. It holds no TXN client data, no cardholder data and no TXN credentials, and it has no access to TxnGlobal.API. Where it runs an example API call, it calls a mock API. Novosapien will not change this scope without TXN's prior written agreement, and a change to it requires an updated Annex A.

1.3 Relationship to the parties' other agreements. This Agreement stands on its own and takes effect whether or not the Master Service Agreement between the parties is signed. When that Agreement is signed, this Agreement continues to govern the Interim Service and is appended to it as an appendix; the Master Service Agreement's own data processing schedule governs processing under that Agreement, and this Agreement prevails for the Interim Service. Nothing here varies the Master Service Agreement or the Support & SLA Agreement.

1.4 Term. This Agreement starts on the date above and ends when Novosapien has deleted the Interim Service data under clause 10 and confirmed that deletion in writing. Clauses 7, 10, 11 and 12 survive for 12 months after that.

2. Roles and applicable law

2.1 TXN is the controller and Novosapien is the processor for all personal data described in Annex A. Novosapien does not determine the purposes or means of the processing, and does not process the personal data for its own purposes.

2.2 Data Protection Laws means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the EU GDPR), the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and Directive 2002/58/EC as implemented in Cyprus, in each case as applicable to the processing concerned. TXN is established in Cyprus, so the EU GDPR governs TXN's processing as controller. The UK GDPR applies to Novosapien's processing in the United Kingdom. Each party complies with the Data Protection Laws that apply to it.

2.3 TXN's instructions. This Agreement, including Annex A, is TXN's complete documented instruction to Novosapien at the date of signature. Further instructions are given in writing by TXN's named contact. Novosapien tells TXN if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the Data Protection Laws.

2.4 TXN's own obligations. TXN is responsible for the lawful basis for the processing, for the Knowledge Hub privacy notice, for the acceptable use policy that tells visitors not to enter personal data, and for any legitimate interest assessment the Data Protection Laws require. Novosapien gives TXN the factual information TXN needs for each of them.

3. Novosapien's obligations as processor

Novosapien will:

(a) Instructions. Process the personal data only on TXN's documented instructions, unless required to do otherwise by law, in which case Novosapien informs TXN of that requirement before processing unless the law prohibits it;

(b) People. Ensure that everyone it authorises to process the personal data is bound by a written duty of confidentiality and has been instructed on the limits in clause 6.2;

(c) Security. Implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures in Annex B, and keep them appropriate to the risk as Article 32 requires;

(d) Sub-processors. Engage only the sub-processors listed in Annex C, on the terms in clause 4;

(e) Data subject rights. Taking into account the nature of the processing, help TXN by appropriate technical and organisational measures to respond to a data subject exercising their rights, as clause 8 sets out, and pass any request Novosapien receives directly to TXN without responding to it itself;

(f) Assistance. Help TXN meet its obligations under Articles 32 to 36, including security, breach notification and data protection impact assessments, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to Novosapien;

(g) End of processing. On TXN's choice, return or delete the personal data as clause 10 sets out, unless the law requires a copy to be kept; and

(h) Audit. Make available to TXN the information TXN reasonably needs to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, and allow and contribute to audits as clause 11 sets out.

4. Sub-processors

4.1 TXN gives general written authorisation for Novosapien to engage the sub-processors listed in Annex C. Novosapien imposes on each of them, in writing, data protection obligations materially equivalent to those in this Agreement, and remains fully liable to TXN for their performance.

4.2 Change control. Novosapien gives TXN at least 20 Business Days' prior written notice of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor. TXN may object on reasonable grounds related to data protection within that period. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, TXN may terminate this Agreement and the Interim Service on written notice, without penalty, and clause 10 applies.

4.3 A provider used only on failover is still a sub-processor and is listed in Annex C. Novosapien will not route TXN personal data to a provider that is not listed.

5. The AI model

5.1 No training. Novosapien will not use, and will configure its sub-processors so that they do not use, personal data processed under this Agreement to train or improve any foundation model.

5.2 European processing. Model inference runs on the European Union multi-region endpoint. Novosapien will not use a global endpoint. If a model provider ceases to offer a European endpoint, Novosapien will tell TXN before routing any personal data to it and will not do so without TXN's written agreement.

5.3 Zero data retention. Before the Interim Service goes live, Novosapien will disable prompt and response caching on the model project, and will request the model provider's abuse-monitoring exception so that prompts are not retained for abuse monitoring. Caching is disabled from go-live. The abuse-monitoring exception takes effect on the provider's approval; Novosapien will tell TXN in writing if it has not been granted by go-live, and the position then is the provider's standard retention of up to 30 days for that purpose and nothing longer.

5.4 What reaches the model. Only the visitor's question and TXN's public documentation reach the model. No IP address and no browser identifier are sent to the model. Novosapien authenticates to the model provider using short-lived federated credentials, so no long-lived model key exists.

6. Security and access

6.1 Novosapien implements and maintains the technical and organisational measures in Annex B, which describe the measures Novosapien actually operates. Novosapien will not materially reduce them during the term.

6.2 Access is controlled by this Agreement. Novosapien's personnel can technically reach the Azure Front Door logs, which hold visitor IP addresses. Novosapien undertakes that its personnel will not access those logs, and will not attempt to link an IP address, a browser identifier or a chat to an identifiable person, except where it is necessary to investigate a security incident, to diagnose a fault, or to carry out a written instruction from TXN. Every such access is by a named individual, is logged, and is reported to TXN on request. Novosapien will not approach an internet service provider or any third party to identify the holder of an IP address.

6.3 Access to the Interim Service environment is held by authorised Novosapien engineers only. Novosapien supplies the names on request and removes access within one Business Day when it is no longer required.

7. Personal data breach

7.1 Novosapien notifies TXN of a personal data breach affecting personal data processed under this Agreement without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, by email to TXN's named contact and by telephone.

7.2 The notification describes the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. Where Novosapien cannot provide all of it at once, it provides what it has and the rest in phases without further undue delay, so that TXN can meet its own 72-hour obligation under Article 33.

7.3 Novosapien does not notify a supervisory authority or any data subject about a breach affecting TXN personal data unless TXN instructs it to or the law requires it. Novosapien cooperates with TXN's investigation and remediation at no additional charge where the breach arises from the Interim Service.

8. Data subject rights

8.1 The Interim Service has no sign-in and holds no names, email addresses or account records. Novosapien therefore cannot identify a data subject from a name or an email address, and Article 11 applies.

8.2 Where a data subject supplies their chat identifier, and the chat has not yet been deleted under Annex A, Novosapien will delete it, or export it, on TXN's instruction within 5 Business Days.

8.3 Novosapien provides TXN, on request, the information TXN needs to answer a data subject or a supervisory authority about the Interim Service, including the categories of recipient and the retention periods in Annex A.

9. Location and international transfers

9.1 Personal data processed under this Agreement is stored in Ireland and processed by the model in the European Union. Error reporting is processed in Germany. Annex A and Annex C state the locations in full.

9.2 United Kingdom access. Novosapien's engineers access the environment from the United Kingdom. That transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

9.3 If the adequacy decision is suspended, withdrawn, amended or expires, the parties will promptly put in place the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another valid transfer mechanism, together with a transfer risk assessment. Transfers may continue in the meantime to the extent the law allows. Novosapien will not transfer personal data processed under this Agreement outside the United Kingdom or the EEA other than as Annex C sets out.

10. Retention, deletion and the move to Direct Transact hosting

10.1 Novosapien retains and deletes personal data in line with the retention schedule in Annex A. Deletion of chats, checkpoints and session data runs automatically and is not dependent on a request.

10.2 At Production Cutover, and on any earlier termination of this Agreement, Novosapien deletes the Interim Service database, its logs and its backups, and confirms that deletion to TXN in writing within 10 Business Days. If TXN asks before that date, Novosapien first returns the data in a commonly used machine-readable format.

10.3 Where a copy persists in an immutable backup, Novosapien isolates it, applies this Agreement to it, and deletes it on the backup cycle stated in Annex A. Novosapien may retain a copy only where the law requires it, and tells TXN what it has retained and why.

10.4 Long-term documentation store. Where a chat is retained beyond 72 hours to improve TXN's documentation, the browser identifier is stripped before it is written, so the retained record cannot be linked to a browser or a visitor. That store is held in the same European environment.

11. Audit, information and regulatory cooperation

11.1 Novosapien makes available to TXN the information TXN reasonably needs to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, including Annex B, the sub-processor list, the retention configuration and the access records under clause 6.2.

11.2 TXN, its auditors and its competent authorities may audit Novosapien's compliance with this Agreement on 20 Business Days' notice, during business hours, subject to confidentiality, and once in any 12-month period unless a personal data breach or a regulator requires otherwise. TXN bears its own costs. Novosapien cooperates and contributes to the audit.

11.3 Security monitoring. Novosapien forwards the Interim Service security and application logs to Direct Transact's Microsoft Sentinel instance where TXN instructs it and Direct Transact provides the connection.

11.4 DORA. Recognising that TXN is subject to Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Novosapien supplies and keeps updated the information TXN needs for its register of information on ICT third-party providers in relation to the Interim Service, tells TXN where the Interim Service is provided from and before changing that location, and assists TXN with ICT incidents affecting it.

12. Liability

12.1 Neither party excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

12.2 Subject to clause 12.1, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings.

12.3 Subject to clause 12.1, each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at £100,000. That cap reflects the limited scope of the Interim Service in clause 1.2: public documentation only, no client data and no TXN credentials. Novosapien maintains cyber insurance of at least £1,000,000 per claim and professional indemnity insurance of at least £1,000,000, and provides evidence of cover on request.

12.4 One pool, not two. If the Master Service Agreement between the parties is signed, the cap in clause 12.3 is not additional to the data protection cap in that Agreement. A claim arising from the same facts is capped once, at the higher of the two caps.

12.5 Each party is responsible for its own compliance with the Data Protection Laws in its own role, and for any administrative fine imposed on it for its own breach. Novosapien's responsibility for its sub-processors is as clause 4.1 states.

Annex B

Technical and organisational measures

These measures describe what Novosapien operates for the Interim Service. Novosapien hosts the Interim Service, so the infrastructure, backup and resilience measures below are Novosapien's own, not TXN's.

(a) Network and ingress. The Knowledge Hub reaches the assistant over public HTTPS at Azure Front Door Premium. The web application firewall runs in prevention mode, with a rate limit of 120 requests per minute per visitor for the assistant and 60 for the documentation server. Front Door reaches the assistant over Private Link, so the assistant has no public IP address. An outbound firewall blocks every destination that is not on an allow-list.

(b) Authentication. The Knowledge Hub presents a token issued by TXN's authorisation server. The assistant validates the issuer, client identifier, audience and expiry, and accepts calls only from the Knowledge Hub's permitted origins. The assistant authenticates to the model provider with short-lived credentials through workload identity federation, so no long-lived model key exists. All other secrets are held in Azure Key Vault.

(c) Encryption. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher, enforced at Front Door and at the database. Data at rest is encrypted with Microsoft-managed keys. Customer-managed keys are supported by the platform and are not currently enabled.

(d) Access control. Access to the subscription is held by named, authorised Novosapien engineers only, on a least-privilege basis, with no shared accounts and multi-factor authentication enforced. The database is reachable on the private network only. Access is reviewed periodically and removed within one Business Day when no longer required, including for leavers. Azure logs every management action. The access limits in clause 6.2 apply to all of it.

(e) Separation. Development and production environments are separated. TXN personal data is not used in development or test environments.

(f) Secure development. Code changes are reviewed before merge to a protected main branch and deployed through CI/CD. Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities, and critical vulnerabilities in components Novosapien controls are remediated promptly, targeting 14 days for critical severity.

(g) Logging and monitoring. Logs are held in Azure Log Analytics and can be forwarded to Direct Transact's Microsoft Sentinel under clause 11.3. The application logs do not store the visitor's message as typed, and hold no IP addresses and no browser identifiers. Security-relevant logs are retained for 30 days as Annex A states.

(h) Endpoint security. Devices used to deliver the Interim Service have full-disk encryption, screen lock and automatic security updates enabled.

(i) Personnel. All personnel with access to the Interim Service are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive documented security-awareness guidance on joining.

(j) Incident response. Novosapien maintains a documented incident-response process covering identification, containment, remediation and post-incident review, and notifies TXN of a personal data breach within 24 hours as clause 7 requires.

(k) Resilience and backup. Because Novosapien hosts the Interim Service, Novosapien is responsible for its backup and recovery. Database backups are retained for 7 days. Novosapien keeps the environment defined as code and re-deployable.

(l) Governance. Novosapien maintains an information-security programme owned by Brett St Clair (Co-Founder), reviewed at least annually. Novosapien does not hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification, and does not claim to. Physical and environmental security is inherited from Microsoft Azure's certifications.

(m) No degradation and flow-down. Novosapien imposes materially equivalent obligations on its sub-processors and will not materially reduce the measures in this Annex during the term.
