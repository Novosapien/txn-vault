Dated [date] · between Novosapien (Novosapien Global Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 17308181), registered office Aldgate Tower, 2 Leman Street, London E1 8FA) and TXN (TXN Global Limited, a company incorporated in Cyprus (company no. Background. Novosapien built the agentic AI layer for the TXN platform under the Master Service Agreement dated [date]. This Agreement is the managed support and maintenance for that layer after launch. It is a managed service, not a break-fix contract: Novosapien runs the agentic layer, keeps it healthy as TXN's API and documentation change, and responds to issues against the targets in Schedule 2.

1. Definitions

1.1 "Supported System" means the agentic AI layer Deliverables described in Schedule 1. "Support" means the services in clause 3. "Support Tier" means the tier selected in Schedule 2 (Business Hours, Extended, or Mission Critical). "Service Hours" means the human-response hours of the selected Support Tier as set out in Schedule 2. "Incident" means a failure of the Supported System to operate materially as intended. "Priority" means the severity of an Incident under Schedule 2. "Availability" means the percentage of time the Supported System is available in a calendar month, measured under Schedule 2. "Support Allocation" means the monthly hours in clause 3.4. "Business Day" means a day, other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday, when banks in London are open for business.

1.2 Automated monitoring and alerting under clause 3.1 runs continuously (24/7) at every Support Tier. The Support Tier determines when Novosapien's team responds.

2. Term and charges

2.1 The "Launch Date" is the day the agentic AI layer is officially deemed launched under the Master Service Agreement, as confirmed in writing by the parties. This Agreement starts on the Launch Date and runs for an initial term of [12] months, then continues monthly until ended under clause 9.3. The support fee in clause 2.2 begins only when the Phase 3 team retainer under the Master Service Agreement ends; while that retainer is running, its monthly charge covers support and no separate support fee is payable.

2.2 The support fee for the selected Support Tier is set out in Schedule 3, invoiced monthly in advance and exclusive of VAT. Novosapien may review the fee at each anniversary on 60 days' notice, with any increase capped at the change in the UK Consumer Prices Index since the last review plus [3]%. Interest on late payment accrues at 6% above the Bank of England base rate.

3. What the fee covers

3.1 Run and monitor. Novosapien's Agent Ops platform watches model usage, cost, agent performance, and anomalies across the full agent fleet, with proactive alerting before issues reach users.

3.2 Agentic incident response. Novosapien's monitoring agents observe the Supported System's error logs and telemetry continuously (24/7, at every Support Tier). When an error is detected:

(a) an agent first attempts automated remediation, which may include applying a known fix or rolling back the change that caused the error, within the agent's authorised scope and without bypassing the approval and permission model in the Master Service Agreement;

(b) if automated remediation does not resolve the error, the agent escalates it as an Incident: it notifies a human through the Support Channels, being the shared Slack channel between the parties and Novosapien's support email address support@novosapien.ai (together the "Support Channels", both available to both parties), assigns a provisional Priority under Schedule 2, and includes the diagnostic detail available, including the error, the affected components, the remediation attempted and its result, and the suspected cause, so that a human can decide how to resolve it;

(c) a provisional Priority may be re-graded on human review, and Novosapien records automated remediations and escalations in the monthly service report (clause 3.7). Response targets under Schedule 2 run from the escalation notification in (b), or from TXN's report under clause 3.5, whichever is earlier.

3.3 Keep healthy. Novosapien maintains the Supported System as TXN's Core API and documentation change, including prompt tuning, guardrail adjustments, and small enhancements.

3.4 Support Allocation. The fee includes up to 20 hours per month of keep-healthy and continuous-improvement work under clauses 3.1 and 3.3. Incident response to the targets in Schedule 2 (clauses 3.2 and 3.5) is part of the managed service and does not consume the Support Allocation; a persistent pattern of Incidents is addressed through the service reviews in clause 3.7, not charged by the hour. Unused hours [do not roll over].

3.5 Incident response. During Service Hours, Novosapien responds to and resolves Incidents through the Support Channels to the Priority targets in Schedule 2. Resolution includes a reasonable workaround that restores material functionality, with a permanent fix to follow.

3.6 Change requests. Work beyond the Support Allocation, or any new scope, is agreed as a change request and charged at Novosapien's standard day rates in Schedule 3.

3.7 Reporting and reviews. Novosapien provides a monthly service report (Incidents, automated remediations and escalations, response performance, Availability, Support Allocation used). At the Extended tier the parties hold a quarterly service review; at the Mission Critical tier, a monthly service review. At every tier, Novosapien provides a written post-incident report within 5 Business Days of resolving any Priority 1 Incident, covering root cause, impact, remediation, and prevention.

3.8 Tier changes. TXN may change Support Tier on 30 days' written notice, effective from the next billing month, at the charges agreed for the new tier. A downgrade below the tier TXN's regulatory obligations require needs TXN's written confirmation.

4. Availability

4.1 Novosapien targets the monthly Availability of the selected Support Tier (Schedule 2) for the parts of the Supported System within its control, measured under Schedule 2.

4.2 Availability excludes the matters in clause 7 (agreed maintenance, third-party or model-provider outages, TXN-caused or partner-caused issues, and force majeure).

5. TXN obligations

5.1 TXN reports Incidents through the Support Channels with enough detail to reproduce them, gives Novosapien the access and information it reasonably needs, keeps its own systems and credentials in order, and uses the Supported System within its documented scope. Response and resolution targets pause while Novosapien is waiting on TXN or another partner for information or access.

6. Service performance

6.1 Novosapien monitors its performance against the targets in Schedule 2 and reports it monthly under clause 3.7. Missing a target in a given month is not of itself a material breach of this Agreement, but the chronic-failure right in Schedule 2 applies, and any claim for service failures is subject to the liability cap in clause 9.2. No service credits apply under this Agreement.

7. Exclusions

7.1 Novosapien is not responsible for issues caused by: agreed maintenance windows; TXN's own systems, data, or misuse; changes TXN or another partner made outside change control; the unavailability or failure of third-party services, hosting, or AI model providers whose availability Novosapien does not control; or force majeure. Materially degraded AI output is not excluded on this ground: it is handled as an Incident under clause 8.1 and Schedule 2.

7.2 In particular, the TXN Platform's hosting, infrastructure, and multi-tenancy architecture are provided and managed by TXN or its other partners, not by Novosapien (clause 4.6 of the Master Service Agreement). A failure or degradation of the Supported System caused by them is not an Incident caused by Novosapien.

7.3 For the matters in this clause 7, downtime they cause is excluded from Availability measurement and does not count toward chronic failure, and Novosapien has no liability for them (clause 9.2). For hosting, TXN-caused, and partner-caused matters, the response and resolution targets also do not apply. For AI model-provider unavailability, Novosapien still owes the Schedule 2 response target and will use reasonable endeavours to mitigate, including failover to an alternative model where the architecture allows; the resolution target and Availability measurement are suspended only for the period of the provider's unavailability.

8. AI-specific support terms

8.1 The Supported System uses generative and agentic AI whose output is generated and probabilistic. Materially degraded output quality is handled as an Incident under the Priority definitions, but Novosapien does not warrant that AI output is accurate, complete, or fit for a particular purpose, and TXN keeps appropriate human oversight for material decisions, consistent with the "advise, do not decide" model.

8.2 Where the Supported System depends on a third-party model provider, Novosapien uses reasonable endeavours to maintain service and manages provider changes under change control, but is not liable for the provider's failures (clause 7).

8.3 Security and regulatory incidents. Novosapien notifies TXN without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours, on becoming aware of a personal-data breach or material security incident affecting the Supported System, with the information TXN needs for its own notification obligations (including the 72-hour window under the GDPR), and gives TXN reasonable assistance with its regulatory incident reporting, including major-incident reporting under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) where it applies to TXN.

8.4 Regulatory cooperation. Clauses 4.4 (Regulatory cooperation), 4.4A (DORA termination rights), and 4.4B (Critical or important function) of the Master Service Agreement apply to this Agreement as they apply to that agreement, with references to the Services read as references to the Support and the Supported System.

9. Data protection, liability, and general

9.1 Where Novosapien processes personal data to provide Support, it acts as processor and TXN as controller, under Schedule 4 (Data Processing), which meets Article 28 of the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR as applicable. Any transfer of personal data outside the UK or the EEA is made only under a valid transfer mechanism (EU SCCs and/or UK IDTA/UK Addendum).

9.2 Neither party excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud, or for anything that cannot lawfully be limited. Subject to that, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, and each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the greater of £50,000 and the total charges paid under this Agreement in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim. This cap is separate from, and not shared with, the caps under the Master Service Agreement, which apply only to that agreement and its SoWs. The cap and the exclusion of indirect loss do not apply to a breach of confidentiality or to wilful misconduct. Novosapien has no liability for the matters excluded in clause 7 (including the TXN Platform's hosting, infrastructure, and multi-tenancy architecture managed by TXN or its other partners), and nothing arising from them counts toward the cap.

9.3 After the initial term in clause 2.1, either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. During the initial term, either party may terminate only for the other's uncured material breach ([14] days' notice to cure), including chronic failure under Schedule 2, or on the other's insolvency. On termination Novosapien gives reasonable transition assistance at the day rates in Schedule 3 and returns or deletes TXN's data. Confidentiality, data protection, and liability survive.

9.4 General terms (entire agreement excluding fraud; variation in writing; no third-party rights; independent contractors; no waiver by delay; severance; counterparts and electronic signature) apply. Novosapien maintains the insurance described in the Master Service Agreement throughout the term of this Agreement. Notices are given in writing, by email or letter: to Novosapien at info@novosapien.ai or its registered office; to TXN at [TXN notice email] or its registered office; an email notice is deemed received on the next Business Day after sending, unless the sender receives a delivery-failure message. Part C: Technical and organisational measures

The Supported System runs in production on TXN's own infrastructure (Microsoft Azure, engaged on TXN's account, per clause 4.6 of the Master Service Agreement). The measures below cover what Novosapien controls: its access, tooling, development practice, and personnel.

(a) Governance. Novosapien maintains an information-security programme owned by Brett St Clair (Co-Founder), with security practices reviewed at least annually. Novosapien does not currently hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification; it commits to pursuing alignment with ISO 27001 controls and will discuss a certification roadmap with TXN in good faith.

(b) Access control. Access to systems processing TXN data is granted on a least-privilege, role-based basis to named individual accounts, with no shared accounts for production access. Multi-factor authentication is enforced on all systems used to provide Support. Credentials are held in a password manager; production secrets live in the hosting platform's managed secret store, never in source code. Access is reviewed periodically and revoked within one Business Day when no longer required, including for leavers.

(c) Encryption. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent via the hosting platform's managed encryption.

(d) Infrastructure. Physical and environmental security is inherited from Microsoft Azure's certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2). Development and production environments are separated; TXN personal data is not used in development or test environments.

(e) Secure development. Code changes are reviewed before merge to a protected main branch and deployed through CI/CD. Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities, and critical vulnerabilities in components Novosapien controls are remediated promptly, targeting 14 days for critical severity.

(f) Logging and monitoring. Production systems emit centralised logs; administrative and support access to systems holding TXN data is logged. Security-relevant logs are retained for at least 90 days.

(g) Endpoint security. Devices used to provide Support have full-disk encryption, screen lock, and automatic security updates enabled.

(h) Personnel. All personnel with access to TXN data are bound by written confidentiality obligations. Documented security-awareness onboarding is being implemented and will be in place before this Agreement commences.

(i) Incident response. Novosapien maintains a documented incident-response process covering identification, containment, remediation, and post-incident review, and notifies TXN of a personal-data breach affecting TXN data within 24 hours of becoming aware, with the information TXN needs for its own 72-hour controller obligations (clause 8.3).

(j) Resilience and backup. Backup and disaster recovery for the TXN Platform and its data stores are TXN's responsibility (clause 4.6 of the Master Service Agreement). Novosapien keeps its production configuration defined as code and re-deployable, and takes periodic precautionary snapshots of the configuration and artefacts it controls, without assuming responsibility for TXN's backup regime.

(k) No degradation. Novosapien flows materially equivalent obligations down to its own sub-processors and will not materially degrade the measures in this Part C during the term.
