Support & Service Level Agreement

Dated             2026 · between Novosapien (Novosapien Global Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company no. 17308181, registered office Aldgate Tower, 2 Leman Street, London E1 8FA) and TXN (TXN Global Limited, a company incorporated in Cyprus, company no.         , registered office Office 303, 20 Iacovou Patatsou, Egkomi, 2408 Nicosia, Cyprus).

Background. Novosapien built the agentic AI layer for the TXN platform under the Master Service Agreement between the parties. This Agreement is the managed support and maintenance for that layer. It is a managed service, not a break-fix contract: Novosapien runs or supports each component, keeps it healthy as TXN's Core API and documentation change, and responds to Incidents against the targets in Schedule 2.

Components arrive in support at different times and are hosted differently. Novosapien currently hosts the Knowledge Hub AI Assistant in its own cloud subscription. The Console components run in the TXN Production Environment, which Direct Transact operates and is the only party that may deploy to. This Agreement handles both, and the move from one to the other, expressly.

1. Definitions

1.1 "Supported System" means the components listed in Schedule 1, each from its own Service Commencement Date. "Supported Component" means any one of them. "Support" means the services in clause 3. "Support Tier" means the tier selected in Schedule 2. "Service Hours" means the human-response hours of the selected Support Tier. "Incident" means a failure of a Supported Component to operate materially as intended. "Priority" means the severity of an Incident under Schedule 2. "Availability" means the percentage of time a Supported Component in Hosted Mode is available in a calendar month, measured under Schedule 2. "Support Allocation" means the monthly hours in clause 3.4. "Business Day" means a day, other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, when banks in London are open for business. "AI Model Services", "TXN Production Environment" and "Production Cutover" have the meanings given to them in clause 1.2 of the Master Service Agreement.

1.2 Automated monitoring and alerting under clause 3.1 run continuously, 24 hours a day, at every Support Tier. The Support Tier determines when Novosapien's people respond.

2. Commencement, term and charges

2.1 Commencement. This Agreement starts on the Commencement Date, being the date the Knowledge Hub AI Assistant first operates in live service for TXN's users, confirmed in writing by both parties. It runs for an initial term of 12 months from that date, then continues monthly until ended under clause 11.3.

2.2 Components arrive in support one at a time. Each Supported Component is supported from its own Service Commencement Date: the date that component first operates in live service in the TXN Production Environment and the parties confirm it in writing. No date is fixed for the Console components in Schedule 1 Part B, because the date TXN takes them live is not yet set. A component is not supported, and carries no service level, before its Service Commencement Date. The parties record each confirmed date in the monthly service report, which is then the agreed record of what is in support.

2.3 Support fee. The support fee for the selected Support Tier is set out in Schedule 3, invoiced monthly in advance and exclusive of VAT. No support fee is separately payable for any month in which TXN is also paying the Phase 2 team charge, or an agreed Phase 3 retainer, under the Master Service Agreement: those charges cover support for the months they run. The support fee begins with the first month in which neither is payable. Novosapien may review the fee at each anniversary on 60 days' notice, with any increase capped at the change in the UK Consumer Prices Index since the last review plus 3%. Interest on late payment accrues at 6% above the Bank of England base rate.

2.4 Pass-through costs. For a Supported Component in Hosted Mode, Novosapien holds the cloud, AI model and observability accounts and pays for them. Novosapien recharges those costs to TXN at the amount billed to Novosapien plus a 10% administration fee, invoiced monthly in arrears with a copy of the underlying provider statements. Novosapien tells TXN before any material change in the run rate. No pass-through applies to a component in Supplier Mode, where TXN holds the provider accounts directly and pays them. This clause governs the recharge of these costs; where it differs from clause 3.7(c) of the Master Service Agreement, this clause prevails.

3. What the fee covers

3.1 Run and monitor. Novosapien's Agent Ops platform watches model usage, cost, agent performance and anomalies across the agent fleet, with proactive alerting before issues reach users.

3.2 Agentic incident response. Novosapien's monitoring agents observe the Supported System's error logs and telemetry continuously, at every Support Tier. When an error is detected:

(a) an agent first attempts automated remediation, which may include applying a known fix or rolling back the change that caused the error, within the agent's authorised scope and without bypassing the approval and permission model in the Master Service Agreement;

(b) if automated remediation does not resolve the error, the agent escalates it as an Incident: it notifies a human through the Support Channels, being the shared Slack channel between the parties and Novosapien's support email address support@novosapien.ai, both available to both parties; assigns a provisional Priority under Schedule 2; and includes the diagnostic detail available, including the error, the affected components, the remediation attempted and its result, and the suspected cause;

(c) a provisional Priority may be re-graded on human review. Response targets run from the escalation notification in (b), or from TXN's report under clause 3.5, whichever is earlier.

3.3 Keep healthy. Novosapien maintains the Supported System as TXN's Core API and documentation change, including prompt tuning, guardrail adjustments and small enhancements.

3.4 Support Allocation. The fee includes up to 20 hours per month of keep-healthy and continuous-improvement work under clauses 3.1 and 3.3. Incident response to the targets in Schedule 2 is part of the managed service and does not consume the Support Allocation; a persistent pattern of Incidents is addressed through the service reviews in clause 3.7, not charged by the hour. Unused hours do not roll over.

3.5 Incident response. During Service Hours, Novosapien responds to and resolves Incidents through the Support Channels to the Priority targets in Schedule 2. What resolution means depends on the operating mode of the affected component: see clause 4.3.

3.6 Change requests. Work beyond the Support Allocation, or any new scope, is agreed as a change request and charged at the day rates in Schedule 3.

3.7 Reporting and reviews. Novosapien provides a monthly service report: Incidents, automated remediations and escalations, response performance, Availability for Hosted Mode components, Support Allocation used, paused-clock periods under clause 4.4, break-glass access grants under clause 4.5, pass-through costs under clause 2.4, and the confirmed Service Commencement Dates. At the Extended tier the parties hold a quarterly service review; at Mission Critical, a monthly one. At every tier, Novosapien provides a written post-incident report within 5 Business Days of resolving any Priority 1 Incident, covering root cause, impact, remediation and prevention.

3.8 Tier changes. TXN may change Support Tier on 30 days' written notice, effective from the next billing month, at the charges agreed for the new tier. A downgrade below the tier TXN's regulatory obligations require needs TXN's written confirmation.

4. How Novosapien supports each component

Who hosts a component decides what Novosapien can actually do about it. This clause states the two modes, and what each one means for resolution, for the clock and for access.

4.1 Two operating modes. Each Supported Component is supported in one of two modes, identified for each component in Schedule 1:

(a) Hosted Mode. Novosapien hosts and runs the component in its own cloud subscription. Novosapien controls the infrastructure, and is therefore responsible for its availability, its backup and recovery, and the security of the stack it runs on. At the date of this Agreement the Knowledge Hub AI Assistant is the only component in Hosted Mode.

(b) Supplier Mode. TXN or Direct Transact hosts and runs the component in the TXN Production Environment. Direct Transact controls all access and is the only party that may deploy to it. Novosapien supplies the software, the fixes, the releases, the runbooks, the release evidence and third-line expertise. Novosapien does not operate the component and holds no standing access to it.

4.2 Moving between modes. A component moves from Hosted Mode to Supplier Mode at its Production Cutover, confirmed in writing by both parties. Until the parties confirm it, the component stays in Hosted Mode, and nothing elsewhere in this Agreement displaces that for any period in which it is not actually the case.

4.3 What resolution means in each mode. In Hosted Mode, an Incident is resolved when Novosapien restores material functionality, whether by a fix or by a reasonable workaround, with a permanent fix to follow. In Supplier Mode, Novosapien cannot deploy, so an Incident is resolved when Novosapien delivers to TXN's release process a tested fix, or a workaround that restores material functionality, together with the release notes and test evidence that process requires. The resolution target is met on that delivery. Deployment, and the timing of deployment, are TXN's and Direct Transact's, and the time they take is not counted against Novosapien.

4.4 When the clock pauses. Response and resolution targets pause while Novosapien is waiting on TXN or another partner for information, for a decision, for access under clause 4.5, or for a release approval or deployment slot. Each pause is recorded in the monthly service report with the time it started and ended, so the measurement is auditable rather than asserted.

4.5 Break-glass access. In Supplier Mode Novosapien holds no standing access. Where diagnosing or fixing an Incident genuinely requires access to the TXN Production Environment, or to data held in it, Novosapien requests that access in writing through the Support Channels, naming the individual who needs it, the systems concerned, the purpose, and the period it expects to need. TXN or Direct Transact grants it for a defined period and withdraws it at the end of that period. Each grant and withdrawal is logged by both parties and reported under clause 3.7. Novosapien accesses only what the Incident requires. The data protection consequence is set out in clause 10.3.

5. Availability

5.1 Novosapien commits to the monthly Availability target of the selected Support Tier (Schedule 2) for Supported Components in Hosted Mode only, measured under Schedule 2.

5.2 No availability commitment in Supplier Mode. For a component in Supplier Mode, TXN or Direct Transact hosts it, operates it and deploys to it. Novosapien therefore gives no Availability target and no Availability measurement for it, and no Availability-based remedy or termination right arises in respect of it. What Novosapien commits to for those components is the response and resolution targets in Schedule 2, as clause 4.3 defines them.

5.3 Availability excludes the matters in clause 8 (agreed maintenance, third-party or model-provider outages, TXN-caused or partner-caused issues, and force majeure).

6. TXN obligations

6.1 TXN reports Incidents through the Support Channels with enough detail to reproduce them; gives Novosapien the access and information it reasonably needs, including deciding break-glass requests under clause 4.5 without undue delay; keeps its own systems and credentials in order; and uses the Supported System within its documented scope.

6.2 For Supplier Mode components, TXN is responsible for operating the TXN Production Environment, for its availability, backup and recovery, for its security and access control, and for deploying the fixes and releases Novosapien delivers. TXN procures the same from Direct Transact.

7. Service performance

7.1 Novosapien monitors its performance against the targets in Schedule 2 and reports it monthly under clause 3.7. Missing a target in a given month is not of itself a material breach, but the chronic-failure right in Schedule 2 applies, and any claim for service failures is subject to the liability cap in clause 11.2. No service credits apply under this Agreement.

8. Exclusions

8.1 Novosapien is not responsible for issues caused by: agreed maintenance windows; TXN's own systems, data or misuse; changes TXN or another partner made outside change control; the unavailability or failure of third-party services, hosting or AI model providers whose availability Novosapien does not control; or force majeure. Materially degraded AI output is not excluded on this ground: it is handled as an Incident under clause 9.1 and Schedule 2.

8.2 The TXN Production Environment. For Supplier Mode components, the hosting, infrastructure and multi-tenancy architecture are provided and managed by TXN or its other partners, not by Novosapien (clause 4.6 of the Master Service Agreement). A failure or degradation caused by them is not an Incident caused by Novosapien. This clause does not apply to a component in Hosted Mode, where Novosapien hosts and is responsible.

8.3 For the matters in this clause 8, downtime they cause is excluded from Availability measurement, does not count toward chronic failure, and Novosapien has no liability for them (clause 11.2). For hosting, TXN-caused and partner-caused matters, the response and resolution targets also do not apply. For AI model-provider unavailability, Novosapien still owes the Schedule 2 response target and will use reasonable endeavours to mitigate, including failover to an alternative model where the architecture allows; the resolution target and Availability measurement are suspended only for the period of the provider's unavailability.

8.4 Detection and alerting. Novosapien supports the Agent Inbox, being the surface that receives an alert, explains it, proposes an action and executes it on approval. Detection, monitoring and thresholds sit with Direct Transact and the Stackworx Console. A failure of those systems, or of the feed that carries alerts and change events into the Agent Inbox, is not an Incident under this Agreement and is excluded from every service level, Availability measurement and response or resolution target.

9. AI-specific support terms

9.1 The Supported System uses generative and agentic AI whose output is generated and probabilistic. Materially degraded output quality is handled as an Incident under the Priority definitions, but Novosapien does not warrant that AI output is accurate, complete or fit for a particular purpose, and TXN keeps appropriate human oversight for material decisions, consistent with the advise-do-not-decide model.

9.2 Where the Supported System depends on a third-party model provider, Novosapien uses reasonable endeavours to maintain service and manages provider changes under change control, but is not liable for the provider's failures (clause 8).

9.3 Security and regulatory incidents. In every operating mode, Novosapien notifies TXN without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours, on becoming aware of a personal data breach or material security incident affecting the Supported System, with the information TXN needs for its own notification obligations including the 72-hour window under the GDPR, and gives TXN reasonable assistance with its regulatory incident reporting, including major-incident reporting under Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) where it applies to TXN.

9.4 Regulatory cooperation. Clauses 4.4 (Regulatory cooperation), 4.4A (DORA termination rights) and 4.4B (Critical or important function) of the Master Service Agreement apply to this Agreement as they apply to that agreement, with references to the Services read as references to the Support and the Supported System.

Novosapien processes only what the Incident requires, deletes any personal data it extracted for the diagnosis when the Incident is closed, and confirms the deletion in the monthly service report.

Part C: Technical and organisational measures

The measures below cover what Novosapien controls: its access, its tooling, its development practice and its personnel. For a Hosted Mode component Novosapien also controls the infrastructure, and Annex B of the Data Processing Agreement (Interim Hosting) states the measures that apply to the environment it hosts.

(a) Governance. Novosapien maintains an information-security programme owned by Brett St Clair (Co-Founder), reviewed at least annually. Novosapien does not hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification, and does not claim to.

(b) Access control. Access to systems processing TXN data is granted on a least-privilege, role-based basis to named individual accounts, with no shared accounts and multi-factor authentication enforced on all systems used to provide Support. Credentials are held in a password manager; secrets live in a managed secret store, never in source code. Access is reviewed periodically and removed within one Business Day when no longer required, including for leavers. Break-glass access is additionally time-boxed and logged under clause 4.5.

(c) Encryption. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent via the hosting platform's managed encryption.

(d) Separation. Development and production environments are separated. TXN personal data is not used in development or test environments.

(e) Secure development. Code changes are reviewed before merge to a protected main branch and released through CI/CD. Dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities, and critical vulnerabilities in components Novosapien controls are remediated promptly, targeting 14 days for critical severity.

(f) Logging and monitoring. Administrative and support access to systems holding TXN data is logged. Security-relevant logs are retained for at least 90 days.

(g) Endpoint security. Devices used to provide Support have full-disk encryption, screen lock and automatic security updates enabled.

(h) Personnel. All personnel with access to TXN data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive documented security-awareness guidance on joining.

(i) Incident response. Novosapien maintains a documented incident-response process covering identification, containment, remediation and post-incident review, and notifies TXN of a personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware (clause 9.3).

(j) Resilience and backup. For a Supplier Mode component, backup and disaster recovery are TXN's responsibility (clause 6.2). For a Hosted Mode component they are Novosapien's (clause 4.1(a)). Novosapien keeps its own configuration defined as code and re-deployable.

(k) No degradation. Novosapien flows materially equivalent obligations down to its own sub-processors and will not materially reduce the measures in this Part C during the term.
