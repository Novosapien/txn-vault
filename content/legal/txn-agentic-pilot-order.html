Agentic Pilot Order
Standalone interim agreement for the six-week agentic pilot

Dated between Novosapien (Novosapien Global Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company no. 17308181, registered office Aldgate Tower, 2 Leman Street, London E1 8FA) and TXN (TXN Global Limited, incorporated in Cyprus, company no. , registered office Office 303, 20 Iacovou Patatsou, Egkomi, 2408 Nicosia, Cyprus).

Background. TXN is committed to a wider engagement under which Novosapien designs and builds the agentic AI layer for the TXN platform, to be governed by a Master Service Agreement (the "MSA") the parties are finalising. TXN wants the six-week agentic pilot to start now, ahead of signing the MSA. This Order is a standalone, interim contract that lets the pilot start and completes on its own terms. It is not conditional on the MSA. When the MSA is signed, clause 8 governs how the pilot is carried into it.

Terms

1. The Pilot.

1.1 Novosapien provides the Pilot and delivers the Deliverables described in Schedule 1, with reasonable skill and care. Novosapien builds against mocked endpoints and synthetic or test data so the Pilot proves the agentic experience without depending on TXN's other vendors.

1.2 The Pilot is complete when Novosapien demonstrates the Deliverables to TXN and hands over the pilot work product (source, prompts, configurations, and the simulation harness) in a documented state. TXN has 5 Business Days from that hand-over to accept, or to reject in writing giving reasons where a Deliverable materially fails to meet Schedule 1. If TXN does not reject within that period, the Pilot is treated as accepted. Novosapien will correct a valid rejection and re-submit.

2. Charges and payment.

2.1 The charge for the Pilot is £54,187.50, exclusive of VAT. VAT is added where it applies.

2.2 Novosapien invoices 50% on signature of this Order and 50% on completion of the Pilot under clause 1.2. Each valid invoice is payable on its date of issue. Interest on late payment accrues at 6% above the Bank of England base rate. If an undisputed invoice is overdue, Novosapien may suspend the Pilot on 5 Business Days' further written notice.

3. Intellectual property. This clause is the heart of this Order and is intended to be unambiguous.

3.1 What TXN owns. The agents, models, data, source code, prompts, configurations, and documentation that Novosapien builds specifically for TXN in the Pilot are the "Pilot Deliverables" and their intellectual property is "Foreground IP". Novosapien assigns to TXN, with full title guarantee, all Foreground IP in the Pilot Deliverables as and when it is created, by way of present assignment of both existing and future rights. Novosapien will do anything TXN reasonably asks, at TXN's cost, to perfect that assignment.

3.2 Assignment is not conditional on payment. Title to the Foreground IP passes as it is created, whether or not any charge has been paid. Novosapien's protection for unpaid charges is its right to suspend the Pilot under clause 2.2, not any right to withhold, reclaim, or delay title.

3.3 Novosapien Background IP. Novosapien keeps its Background IP: its own workforce of delivery agents, and the agent frameworks, orchestration patterns, prompt libraries, evaluation and simulation tooling, models, and general know-how it uses to deliver the Pilot. These are Novosapien's, are part of its cost of delivery, are not a Pilot Deliverable, and are not assigned. Novosapien licenses its Background IP to TXN only as embedded in the Pilot Deliverables and as needed to use them. Novosapien may reuse its general skills, methods, and generic components, provided it does not use TXN's Confidential Information or the Pilot Deliverables.

3.4 Any third-party or open-source components in the Pilot Deliverables are passed through to TXN under their own licences.

4. Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's Confidential Information confidential and uses it only for the Pilot, except as required by law or disclosed to its advisers. This clause survives for 2 years after the Pilot ends.

5. Data protection.

5.1 The parties intend the Pilot to run on synthetic or test data so far as practicable. Cardholder data is out of scope for the Pilot.

5.2 To the extent Novosapien processes personal data on TXN's behalf during the Pilot (for example within Internal Ops v1, such as meeting capture or customer-onboarding material), Novosapien acts as processor and TXN as controller, and Novosapien processes that personal data only on TXN's documented instructions. For that processing the parties adopt, and treat as set out in this Order, the data-processing terms in Schedule 3 (Data Processing) of the MSA in the form last exchanged between them. TXN's and its clients' data will not be used to train Novosapien's or any third party's foundation models.

To confirm before signature (see reviewer brief, Q1): whether the Pilot will process any real personal data, or run on synthetic or test data only. If synthetic only, clause 5.2 can be simplified to a statement to that effect. The draft covers both cases so the Pilot is protected either way.

6. Warranties and liability.

6.1 Novosapien warrants it provides the Pilot with reasonable skill and care. Except as stated, and to the extent permitted by law, all other warranties are excluded. Software and AI output are not warranted to be error-free or uninterrupted; the Pilot runs on simulated tools and synthetic data and is a demonstration, not a production system.

6.2 Neither party excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

6.3 Subject to clause 6.2, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue, goodwill, or anticipated savings.

6.4 Subject to clause 6.2, each party's total liability under this Order is capped at the greater of £150,000 and the total charges paid under this Order. A separate, higher cap of the greater of £250,000 and that general cap applies to a breach of the Data Protection Laws or of clause 5. These caps and the exclusion in clause 6.3 do not apply to a breach of confidentiality (clause 4) or to wilful misconduct. These caps and exclusions match the MSA, so nothing changes when the Pilot is carried into the MSA under clause 8.

7. Term. This Order starts on the Start date and runs for the six-week Pilot and until the charges are paid. Either party may end it immediately if the other materially breaches and does not cure within 14 days of written notice, or on the other's insolvency. On termination, TXN pays for work performed and work in progress up to termination; Novosapien hands over the Pilot Deliverables completed to that point in a documented state; and clauses 3, 4, 5, 6, and 8 survive.

8. Relationship to the MSA. This clause makes the Pilot fit cleanly into the wider engagement.

8.1 This Order is a complete, standalone agreement for the Pilot. It does not depend on the MSA and continues to govern the Pilot if the MSA is never signed.

8.2 The parties intend to sign the MSA. When they do, the MSA governs the parties' relationship from its date, and the parties will treat the Pilot as the completed Statement of Work 1 under the MSA. The MSA's Schedule 2 (Charges) will record the £54,187.50 Pilot charge as already contracted and paid under this Order, with no double-charging. The wider engagement (the remaining build and later phases) is charged under the MSA and its Statements of Work.

8.3 The Foreground IP already assigned to TXN under clause 3 stays assigned to TXN and is not re-done. The Pilot Deliverables become Deliverables under the MSA and take the benefit of the MSA's ongoing terms. Nothing in the Pilot is rebuilt or re-approved because of the transition.

8.4 If this Order and the signed MSA conflict on the Pilot, the MSA prevails from its date, except that the assignment of Foreground IP in clause 3 is preserved.

9. General. This Order is the entire agreement between the parties about the Pilot (but nothing excludes liability for fraud). Variation only in writing signed by both parties. Neither party assigns this Order without the other's consent, not unreasonably withheld. Notices are given in writing, by email or letter: to Novosapien at info@novosapien.ai or its registered office; to TXN at or its registered office. "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England. Electronic signature is accepted. This Order is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

Schedule 1
Pilot scope and Deliverables

The Pilot runs for six weeks on simulated tools and synthetic or test data. Its purpose is to prove the agentic experience TXN is betting on, ahead of the other vendors' surfaces, and to harden a foundation the wider build plugs into. Novosapien delivers the following.

1. Full Agentic Experience showcase. A working, demonstrable interface that takes a goal, plans, renders live UI in the conversation, and acts on approval. It de-risks the hardest component on simulation before any wire-in spend.

2. Agent Access Layer foundations. The MCP tool surface and permission model, running against mocked Core API endpoints built from the API specification and sample payloads. This surfaces gaps in the API contract and the data the agents need, feeding the later wire-in.

3. Internal Ops v1. The low-dependency, CRM-based parts that return value early: the customer-onboarding agent, meeting capture, and the knowledge engine. These need no Core API or Console dependency. See clause 5 of the Order on data.

Outcome. A demonstrable agentic experience TXN can show its market and its board, plus a hardened foundation (tool surface, permission model, simulation harness) that the rest of the build plugs into.

Acceptance. The Pilot is accepted under clause 1.2 of the Order: on demonstration of the Deliverables above and hand-over of the pilot work product in a documented state, with a 5 Business Day window for TXN to reject in writing with reasons for a material failure to meet this Schedule.

Dependencies and assumptions. The Pilot is built synthetic-first and does not depend on Direct Transact or Stackworkz deliverables. TXN provides timely access to the API specification, sample payloads, brand assets, and the people needed for demonstrations and feedback. Production wire-in to the live Core API, Console, and Data Lake is outside the Pilot and is delivered under the MSA.
