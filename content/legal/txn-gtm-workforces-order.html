Workforce Order 1: Go-to-Market Workforces

Dated   · between Novosapien (Novosapien Global Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 17308181), Aldgate Tower, 2 Leman Street, London E1 8FA) and TXN (TXN Global Limited, a company incorporated in Cyprus (company no.  ), together the parties. This is a Workforce Order under clause 2.3 of the Master Service Agreement between the parties dated   (the "MSA"), which it incorporates. It orders Novosapien's standard AI Workforce products; under MSA clause 2.3 they are licensed, not assigned, and the MSA's acceptance and Foreground-IP provisions do not apply to them.

1. The Workforces ordered

1.1 Content Workforce (Business Audience Lab). A 1–2 day workshop producing four foundational assets (Company Manifesto, Content Pillars, Brand Voice Guidelines, and Visual CI ingestion), then ongoing monthly production of written and visual content across LinkedIn, X, blog, and newsletter at the volume of the selected tier (clause 3.2): idea generation, multi-channel adaptation, a weekly content calendar with scheduling and auto-posting, a dedicated Forward Deploy Engineer, tone and performance tuning workshops, and monthly performance reviews.

1.2 Outbound Workforce (Pipeline Lab). Five specialised AI agents (research, email, LinkedIn, voice, and campaign orchestration) working TXN's target list end to end: a half-day ICP and targeting workshop, email deliverability setup (DKIM, DMARC, SPF), custom voice training on TXN's tone, calendar integration for meeting booking, a dedicated Forward Deploy Engineer, and monthly reviews. The Workforce works from TXN's own lead database, CRM, Sales Navigator, and target lists, plus lead data purchased under clause 3.5.

2. Term and exit

2.1 This Order starts on the kickoff date the parties confirm in writing and each Workforce runs from that date. The Content Workforce runs on the route selected in clause 3.2: the committed programme runs for 12 months and then continues monthly until either party ends it on 30 days' written notice; the pay-as-you-go route is monthly rolling from the start, on the same notice. The Outbound Workforce is monthly rolling: either party may end it at the end of any monthly period on 14 days' written notice.

2.2 Ending one Workforce does not affect the other. MSA clauses on confidentiality, data protection, and liability survive as stated in the MSA.

3. Charges

3.1 Setup, one-time, invoiced at kickoff: Content Workforce £3,000; Outbound Workforce £5,000. Each is covered by the go-live guarantee in clause 4.

3.3 Pay-as-you-go top-ups, stacking within a month: +£500 for 45 pieces; +£1,000 for 95 pieces; +£2,000 for 200 pieces.

3.4 Outbound Workforce fee: £1,500 per month to run the engine (monitoring, tuning, follow-ups, and the monthly review by the Forward Deploy Engineer).

3.5 Outbound usage, passed through at cost plus 10%. AI compute, lead-data purchases (for example Apollo), and voice minutes (for example ElevenLabs) are invoiced monthly in arrears at the amount Novosapien is billed plus an administration fee of 10%, itemised by cost line on the invoice. No lead database is purchased and no campaign run is launched without TXN's prior written approval of the cost. These are the only usage charges; there are no others.

3.6 Invoicing. Setup fees at kickoff; monthly fees monthly in advance; usage monthly in arrears. All figures exclusive of VAT. MSA clause 7.4 (payment, interest, and the limits on suspension) applies.

4. Go-live guarantee

4.1 Each Workforce will be live within 30 days of kickoff: the Content Workforce is live when TXN's channels are publishing approved content on the weekly cadence; the Outbound Workforce is live when approved sequences are running across email, LinkedIn, and voice. If a Workforce is not live within 30 days for reasons within Novosapien's control, Novosapien refunds that Workforce's setup fee in full on TXN's request. This refund is TXN's remedy for late go-live; it does not limit the MSA's liability provisions otherwise.

5. Approvals and trust phases

5.1 Both Workforces start in Phase 1 (human-initiated, human-validated): TXN approves every content piece before publication and every outbound message, sequence, and call script before sending. TXN may elect in writing to progress a Workforce to Phase 2 (agent-initiated, human-validated) and Phase 3 (agent-initiated, agent-validated), and may revert a phase at any time on written notice, effective immediately.

6. Outbound pricing review at day 90

6.1 The Outbound Workforce runs on the clause 3.4–3.5 test-partner structure for the first 90 days of live operation. The parties then review actual volumes and costs together and negotiate go-forward per-lead pricing in good faith, recognising TXN's early-partner economics. Until new pricing is agreed in writing, the clause 3.4–3.5 structure continues; each party's exit right in clause 2.1 is unaffected.

7. Licence, outputs, and brand

7.1 Per MSA clause 2.3, the Workforce products, their agents, and their frameworks remain Novosapien's, licensed to TXN for the term of this Order. TXN owns the outputs generated for it, including published and unpublished content pieces and campaign materials, and all data and brand materials it supplies.

7.2 The custom voice trained on TXN's tone is used only for TXN, and is deactivated and its TXN-specific configuration deleted when the Outbound Workforce ends.

8. Direct-marketing compliance (MSA clause 2.3(e))

8.1 Roles. TXN determines the target market, approves the ICP, the target and suppression lists, and each lead purchase, and is the controller of the prospect personal data processed for outreach; Novosapien operates the Workforce as TXN's processor, on TXN's documented instructions.

8.2 Compliance allocation. TXN is responsible for having a lawful basis for the marketing it instructs, including the corporate-subscriber and consent rules that apply in each target jurisdiction. Novosapien is responsible for executing compliant mechanics: all outreach identifies TXN as the party on whose behalf it is sent; every email carries a working unsubscribe, honoured promptly and applied across channels; suppression lists are maintained and applied before every run; UK voice calls are screened against the TPS/CTPS registers; and the voice agent discloses at the start of each call that it is an AI assistant calling on behalf of TXN, consistent with the transparency obligations of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Calls are recorded or transcribed only where lawful in the destination jurisdiction and as configured with TXN.

8.3 Neither Workforce will be used for outreach to consumers acting in a personal capacity without a prior written variation of this Order addressing the additional consent requirements that entails.
