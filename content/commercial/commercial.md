## Notes for reviewers

- **Two live drafts price the same next stage differently, and only one can go to TXN (24-09).** The **Phase One proposal** covers **2.5 months, 1 October to 15 December, at £90,312.50**, which is exactly the wire-in figure in the Agentic Layer proposal v1.2 and leaves Phase 3 (£108,375) to be proposed separately in December. The engagement total stays at **£270,375**, unchanged. The **Phase Two proposal** of 18 September instead covers **4 months to January at £144,500**, folding Phase 3 in and taking the total to £216,187.50 with no separate Phase 3. **Neither is wrong; they are different commercial shapes.** Phase One is the conservative read: it asks for money already agreed, at a stage boundary TXN can say yes to without re-opening the total. Phase Two asks for a longer commitment and settles the whole remainder in one signature. **Brett to choose before either is sent.**
- **Phase one is scoped as four components, not four workstreams (24-09).** Knowledge hub first (MCP server, then the hub co-pilot, then sandbox assist, the anomaly loop and the email gate), the wire-in second, speed and stability continuous underneath, and the TXN customer onboarding agent closing the pilot's carried-forward scope. The one fixed milestone is the **second week of October** for the knowledge hub AI to be demonstrable. Everything else is sequenced against the partner dependencies. This matches the P1 order on the 24 September flight plan exactly, so the two documents read against each other without contradiction.
- **Phase two is one proposal, four months, October to January (draft v1.0, 18-09).** It replaces the earlier plan of a separate wire-in and a separate Phase 3. The Agentic Layer proposal split the work into a £90,312.50 wire-in to an October launch and a £108,375 Phase 3 from November. The launch moved and the priorities changed, so phase two is now **four committed months at the unchanged £36,125 monthly rate, £144,500**, carrying all four workstreams in parallel. The monthly rate has not moved since the pilot.
- **The order follows TXN's own decision on 16 September.** The knowledge hub is priority one, with the docs MCP server first and the unauthenticated co-pilot behind it. The wire-in to the live platform follows. Stabilisation and performance runs continuously for the full four months rather than as a phase. Phase 3 opens in December. See [[2026-09-16-next-phase-discussion]].
- **One fixed milestone: the second week of October**, for the knowledge hub AI to be demonstrable end to end, which is what was agreed on the 16 September call. Everything else is sequenced against the partner dependencies rather than dated.
- **Register:** the proposal is written for TXN in professional business register, on the established Novosapien proposal format. Delivery defects and internal remediation detail are recorded in [[delivery]] and [[open-questions]], not in the commercial document.

The [[txn-agentic-pilot-order]] is a standalone interim contract for just the pilot: £54,187.50 (1.5 months at the £36,125/mo build rate), 50% on signature / 50% on completion. It is not contingent on the MSA. On MSA signature the pilot becomes the completed **Statement of Work 1**, its charge reconciled in MSA Schedule 2, IP already vested. Consequence: the **MSA's SoW 1 and Schedule 2 need updating** so the build charge reflects the remaining wire-in only (£90,312.50, from £144,500), and the **main agentic-layer proposal is being rebuilt** to show the pilot as done/separate. Top open question: whether the pilot touches real personal data (governs the DP clause).

Ian noted the pricing language as currently written has issues. **Commercially material to Novosapien: Ian said the licence fee is also planned to include the AI elements**, *"initially the plan is that will also include the the AI elements"*. That sets how TXN expects to recover the cost of the agentic layer from its own clients, and is worth a direct conversation. Decision taken not to disclose any numbers in outbound communications.
- **TXN has tied the next phase of Novosapien spend to an honest assessment of delivered value (10-09).** Ian emailed Brett on 9 September asking for Novosapien's view, *"knowing what you're building for us"*, on how TXN should talk about AI, and was explicit that he wants an assessment rather than messaging: *"really how much value and how far have we gone in terms of what we can offer."* The commercial line, verbatim: *"if what we're delivering is not moving the needle enough, then we need to go back and think about how we use the next phase of the Novosapien money to make sure that we are building stuff that does move the needle."* He was equally clear that **TXN is not AI native** and that nothing unbuilt may be claimed as available at launch. It follows his 3 September critique of the interaction model, and it is the most direct statement yet that phase two is conditional. Awaiting Brett's reply. See [[2026-09-10-content-workforce-company-closed]] and [[open-questions]] #88. **Five days later Ian tested the build and said "I just don't think people would use it"** ([[2026-09-15-agentic-standup]]), citing speed and intuitiveness rather than capability, and asking for simple actions to stay in the agent once fast. The value question is now informed by use, and the phase two prioritisation call on 16 September is the natural place to answer it.
