03Included at no charge

Two defects came out of TXN's testing. Both are Novosapien's responsibility and neither is chargeable. They are listed in full because the alternative is that they surface later as a dispute.

Approval gates did not run

Between 8 and 14 September the agent made 68 record-changing writes across nine conversations and rendered no approval cards. The writes included card blocks, card terminations, account closures and spend-control changes. In one case two accounts holding £184,200 and €2,340 were closed after a single confirmation reply.

The governed write is the core safety promise of this design. It held in the pilot build and did not hold in the deployed environment. Novosapien owns this without qualification, and it is the first item of work in October.

The agent is too slow to be worth using

TXN's assessment on 15 September was direct: "so slow", "not remotely intuitive", "I just don't think people would use it", with no defects found. Measured turns in the feedback records run from 22 to 33 seconds, and one flow took 119 seconds to its first action.

The remedy is agreed and started: standard operating procedures that plan once and then execute, composite tools that collapse three or four API calls into one agent turn, and asking for an identifier first rather than retrieving everything. Novosapien will report response times in figures from the next delivery report, because TXN asked for numbers rather than impressions and that is the right test.

Also carried, at no charge

The remaining console feedback41 open records from TXN's UAT sessions across eight themes, covering ceremony on simple actions, lost and stolen card handling, spend-control scope, rendered components and console pages. Each becomes a scoped piece of work
Specification currencyThe agent answered a question about spend controls fluently and incorrectly, because it was running against an out-of-date API specification after Direct Transact moved the specification URLs. Novosapien's retrieval defect, and Novosapien's to fix
